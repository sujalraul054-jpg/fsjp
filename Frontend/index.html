Serial Position Effect

A short memory experiment

The Serial Position Effect

When people study a list, they tend to remember the words at the start and the end far better than the words in the middle. Psychologists call this the serial position effect, and plotted across many attempts it traces a distinctive U-shaped curve.

How this test works

  1. You'll see 10 words, one at a time — a fresh random selection every attempt.
  2. Once the list ends, write down every word you can recall.
  3. We'll score your recall by position and show you the results.
